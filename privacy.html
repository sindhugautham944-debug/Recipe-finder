Privacy, plainly

Your kitchen stays yours.

Good Measure stores your favorites, meal plan, shopping list, and preferences in this browser using local storage. This version does not use accounts, analytics, or advertising trackers.

Your data

Your information stays in the browser on this device. It is not sent to a Good Measure server. Recipe data is bundled with the app. You can export a JSON backup or clear local data in Settings. Clearing browser site data also removes your saved information.

Recipe notes

Dietary tags are informational and may not account for every ingredient or cross-contamination risk. Always read packaging and confirm ingredients yourself, especially when allergies are involved.
